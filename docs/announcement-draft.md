## FOLM 1.2: let New Vegas breathe

**What FOLM is:** a free mod manager for Fallout: New Vegas (with or without Tale of Two Wastelands)
and Fallout 3 on Linux. It installs your mods, puts them in the game, sorts the load order and tells
you in plain words when something is wrong.

**The new goal.** New Vegas can only use 4 GB of memory, however much your PC has. Load it up with
mods and it fills up and crashes; for years the only fix was "use fewer mods". We want to change that.
Instead of crashing when it's full, the game should breathe: FOLM watches how full it is, warns you and
saves your game before it's too late, and step by step moves work out of the game's 4 GB altogether.
Bigger mod setups that stay stable, and hopefully a reason for modders to come back to New Vegas.

**New in this update:**
- **Memory gauge:** see how full the game's 4 GB really is while you play.
- **Saves you before a crash:** when memory gets low, FOLM warns you and has the game save itself, as
  a new save, so you lose minutes, not hours.
- **Crash reports** that say plainly when the game ran out of memory, and what to cut down.
- **Check for updates** for FOLM and your Nexus mods.
- **FAQ and Resources tabs:** answers, guides and modding tools, a click away.
- **Personalize:** FOLM's colors and font, and a Pip-Boy look that takes your in-game colors.
- **Profiles:** rename and delete them in the window; saves always kept.
- Faster with big setups, and fewer false warnings.

**What's next: Second Wind.** A step-by-step plan, done in the open. First, free settings that make
room, and a tool that fits texture packs to your PC. Then **Second Wind**, a free mod for everyone (Windows
too, any mod manager): it pulls in the clutter drawn far away when a fight starts and opens it back up
when it's over, saves your game when memory runs low, and lets other mods ask how much room is left.
After that, a helper program that takes work out of the game's 4 GB altogether. Every step can be
switched off again.

**A Windows edition of FOLM is planned** after that. Most New Vegas players are on Windows, and
Second Wind's parts are Windows programs already, so Windows players get them first. We'll need Windows
testers: if you'd like to help, say so here.

**How it's made.** FOLM is built with an AI coding assistant (Claude, by Anthropic). I decide what it
should do, test it on my own game, and decide what goes in. It's been amazing to see how far that
gets a fan project.

I'm a fan of the series, and I hope this helps revive the modding scene for this wonderful game.
